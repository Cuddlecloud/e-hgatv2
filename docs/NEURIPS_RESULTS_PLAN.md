## 1. Experiment matrix

### Exp A — Optimization scaling (HEADLINE, Req 3/C1)
- **Script:** `scripts/run_benchmark.py` per N.
- **Design:** N ∈ {5, 10, 20, 50}; 30 seeds; 100 gens; pop = 20N; methods =
  {E-HGATv2-NSGA-II (ours), NSGA-II (random), BRKGA}. Oracle PF*: exact at N=5, approx
  (long NSGA-II) at N≥10.
- **Metrics:** final HV (↑), IGD+ (↓), GD+ (↓), Spread (↓), HV/HV*, generations-to-target-HV.
- **Stat test:** 95% bootstrap CIs per method; significance = **non-overlapping CIs** ours vs
  random AND ours vs BRKGA. Plus the **(HV_ours − HV_random) vs N** curve.
- **Acceptance gate:** ours' HV CI lower bound > random's upper bound at N≥10, AND the gap is
  non-decreasing in N (the scale claim). IGD+ likewise.

### Exp B — Surrogate fidelity + faithfulness (Req 2/C2)
- **Script:** `scripts/run_fused_eval.py` (C3 config: core bs256/ep200/lr5e-3, fused
  bs128/ep120/lr4e-3).
- **Design:** uncoupled (done) + coupled pp30; N ∈ {6,10,20,50}; 20 seeds.
- **Metrics:** R²(C_max), R²(E), MAE, **leg/arc critical Jaccard** vs exact TAPE oracle.
- **Stat test:** mean ± 95% Student-t CI over seeds.
- **Acceptance gate:** uncoupled R²(C_max) ≥ 0.95 & leg-Jaccard ≥ 0.90 through N=50 (✓ met);
  coupled R²(C_max) ≥ 0.75 & leg-Jaccard ≥ 0.90 (rework if below).

### Exp C — Faithfulness vs baselines (Req 2/3 → C2/C3)
- **Script:** TAPE vs Sobol vs TreeSHAP comparison (see `scripts/run_landscape.py`,
  `src/ehgat/explain/gnn_landscape.py`; add a head-to-head driver if missing).
- **Metrics:** rank agreement / precision@k of attributed critical tasks **vs the exact
  oracle**; wall-time vs N (scaling).
- **Stat test:** mean ± 95% CI over seeds; TAPE vs each baseline.
- **Acceptance gate:** TAPE precision@1 and rank-corr strictly above both baselines with
  non-overlapping CIs, and TAPE wall-time scales sub-quadratically while Sobol blows up.

## 2. Acceptance criteria (what "NeurIPS-grade" means here)

1. Every headline number reported as **mean ± 95% CI** over ≥ 20 seeds.
2. Every comparative claim backed by a **significance test** (non-overlapping bootstrap CIs
   or Wilcoxon signed-rank), not point estimates.
3. The scale claim (C1) shown as a **monotone curve**, not a single N.
4. No stale/copied numbers (every cell regenerated by the cited run; record run tag + date).
5. Honest caveats kept (coupled has no public benchmark; cascade claim is makespan-only).

### Exp A2 — head-to-head vs the PUBLISHED mp-BRKGA baseline  [status: ◐ running]
_tags: `mp_comparison`, `scripts/run_mp_comparison.py`, 2026-06-22._

**Why:** the prior baseline (`ehgat.baselines.brkga`) is a **single-population** BRKGA — which
is exactly the advisor's *own ablation* (Fontes & Homayouni 2022, Fig. 6), and their full
**multi-population mp-BRKGA** is shown to beat it near the boundaries. Comparing our GNN-guided
search only against the weaker single-pop variant is a rigor gap. We now implement the faithful
**mp-BRKGA** (`ehgat.baselines.mp_brkga`, Sec. 4: Ω single-objective + Π multi-objective
populations, migration pool every `n_ex` gens, biased uniform crossover, paper config
P=20N, Ω=Π=2, n_ex=30) and make it the **primary baseline**.

**Matched-budget protocol (the only fair currency):** mp-BRKGA evaluates (Ω+Π)=4 populations
per generation, so at equal `pop_size` it would get 4× the true evaluations. We therefore give
the single-pop BRKGA and the E-HGATv2 search `pop = 4·(20N)` so **true evaluations per
generation are identical** for all three; same generations; same reconstructed PF\* and HV
reference. The GNN's extra cost is *cheap surrogate (GNN) forward passes for screening*, which
are **not** counted as true evaluations — that is precisely the efficiency being tested.

| Instance (N) | Method | HV / HV\* | GD+ | IGD+ | Spread | true evals |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| toy:5 (5) | E-HGATv2 | 0.9893 | 0.979 | 2.569 | 0.955 | 16,400 |
| toy:5 (5) | mp-BRKGA | 0.9945 | 1.568 | 2.497 | 0.893 | 16,400 |
| toy:5 (5) | single-pop | 0.9956 | 0.734 | 1.458 | 0.890 | 16,400 |

_N=5 is saturated (all ≈0.99) — too trivial to separate methods; the GNN's value is at scale.
**toy:10 and real L15 (N=16) matched-budget runs are in flight** → fill from
`experiments/mp_comparison/mp_comparison_{toy10,L15}.md`._

**Accuracy / faithfulness of the mp-BRKGA re-implementation** (addresses the "equations got
messed up last time" risk — verified by `tests/unit/test_mp_brkga.py`, 10/10 pass):
- **Oracle-bounded:** every mp-BRKGA point is weakly dominated by the exact PF\* at N=5 (it can
  never emit an impossible/too-good objective — the strongest soundness check we have).
- **Boundary behaviour matches the paper:** mp-BRKGA recovers the objective extremes ≥ as well
  as single-pop at matched per-pop budget (replicates Fig. 6, the reason Ω specialists exist).
- Correct eval accounting `(Ω+Π)·P·(G+1)`, determinism, biased-crossover limits (p_e→1 copies
  elite parent, p_e→0 copies the other), Ω=0 degenerate case.
- **External gate (decisive, BLOCKED on advisor data):** reproduce the paper's **Table 2 GD+/Δ**
  on the real **DS01–26** instances. This is the one check that confirms numerical fidelity
  against the published algorithm; everything above is necessary but not sufficient without it.
